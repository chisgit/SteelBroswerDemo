How it works

Step 1: Log in with any credentials (saved to localStorage)

Step 2: Note the profileId from the session panel

Step 3: Close the Steel session

Step 4: Restart Steel with the same profileId

Why? Steel persists the browser profile (heap, DOM, storage) across session boundaries when you provide a profileId.
